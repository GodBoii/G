# Table sound credits

The sounds in `table-sounds.mp3` were edited and mixed for House 37 from recordings made available under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- Card slides, placement, gathering, and chip handling by Kenney. [Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio)
- Roulette rail roll, ball bounces, and pocket impact recorded at the casino in Évian-les-Bains by f_ilippo. [roulette_casino_evian.aif](https://freesound.org/people/f_ilippo/sounds/59194/)
- Upright piano sampled by Simon Dalzell for Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 Community Edition. The table outcome cues pitch and layer this recording. [Upright Piano C♯4](https://freesound.org/people/sgossner/sounds/374286/)
